User guide

The full guide is written with the step screens (development plan, task 1.10).

What this app does

It takes a board through ten steps, B0 to B9, from what AI the company uses to a resolved position on AI. The board advisor facilitates, the general counsel acts as counsel, and Triodian records. Positions are proposed by the board and management only.

It runs in one of two modes. Standalone, for companies outside prudential regulation, produces the whole appetite. Connected, for APRA, ASIC or MAS regulated entities, produces board directions that the AI Risk Appetite engagement must stay within.

It records what the board decides and who proposed it. It does not recommend what the board's position should be. Questions about the company's legal obligations are recorded for counsel.

Getting started

  1. An administrator adds people in Admin and gives each a role.
  2. Triodian or an administrator starts the engagement and completes set-up: company, mode, areas of the business and board dates.
  3. The steps follow as their screens are added.
